## Owner methods and ordering

1. `retain_rejected_terminal(terminal)` accepts only a strict native terminal

   rejected descriptor and pins; caller-supplied matching DTOs are insufficient.
2. A private strict rejected-entry reader validates shape, bindings, original
   before/native material and the exact outcome-specific evidence domains.
3. `checkpoint_rejected(wire)` admits only the exact adjacent C1 delta against
   original captured head/state, within the original active retention scope and
   matching the independently held create-only descriptor pin. The positive
   entry is `retain_rejected_terminal`; an external wire or an existing/orphaned
   descriptor cannot authorize this checkpoint.

The owner must remain registered in the original thread/run lock, original
wave/channel/selection/nonce/context and ordinal-zero scope. Require original
prepared reference and evidence acknowledgement, running run/episode, started

request/plan, workspace, root and live archive/state. Reject active staged marker,
mixed admission, process failure, unknown terminal, prior published/rejected

success retention. Full source/config/native observations occur outside the
publication and short observation locks. Inside locks use only the original
fixed identities, primitive pins and bounded numeric allowance; invoke no
